# BuildArena Construction Challenge S01: To Infinity, and Beyond.

## Objective
Construct a *Besiege* machine through the BuildArena 2.0 MCP using AI agents, then manually pilot the machine in *The Broken Beyond* space sandbox to reach orbit, stay in orbit, and complete as many orbital periods as possible (up to 3 full cycles).

## Inputs & Tools
*   **Game:** Besiege + The Broken Beyond DLC.
*   **AI Interface:** BuildArena 2.0 MCP (Provides the agent with structural building capabilities).
*   **Tracking:** BuildArena MOD (Generates the `trajectory.csv` during human piloting).

## Outputs (Submission Requirements)
A valid submission for the **Build with Agent** track requires the following 6 files uploaded to Kaggle, plus a Writeup:
1.  `machine_raw.bsg` (The untouched machine generated through the MCP)
2.  `build_history.json`
3.  `build_history_full.json`
4.  `machine_tuned.bsg` (The final run machine, with legal human tunings to non-geometry fields like controls)
5.  `trajectory.csv` (Generated by official tracker)
6.  `chat_transcript.md` (For token estimation)
7.  **Kaggle Writeup** following the exact template.
8.  *(Highly Encouraged)* **Video link** of the flight.

## Metric & Evaluation
The scoring formula is highly structured and prioritizes task completion, speed, integrity, and token efficiency.

**Performance Score [0, 100] =**
*   **70% Orbit Progress:** Calculated from valid cumulative angular displacement. Maxes out at 3 full orbital periods (1080 degrees / 6*pi radians).
*   **20% Speed Score:** Rewards reaching the orbital progress faster.
*   **10% Structure Integrity:** Fraction of original tracked blocks still connected to the Starting Block at the end.

**Final Score =**
`max(0, PerformanceScore - 0.20 * CostPenalty) * BuildModeCoefficient`

## Constraints & Rules
*   **No Manual Structural Editing:** In the "Build with Agent" track, manual BSG structure editing is strictly prohibited. The geometry must be exclusively AI-generated via the MCP.
*   **Tuning is Allowed:** After the raw machine is saved, humans may tune legal non-geometry block Data fields, controls, bindings, and timing. 
*   **Vanilla Constraints:** Machines must stay within vanilla game legal parameter ranges (no fancy rescale mods, physical behavior changes).
*   **Cameras:** Adding camera blocks for video recording is explicitly allowed.

## Tracks & Judging Criteria
**Build with Agent (Main Track)**
*   **Autopilot Mode (Coefficient: x1.15):** Prompt up front, no human steering once the official build starts.
*   **Copilot Mode (Coefficient: x1.00):** Human-in-the-loop natural language guidance during the build.

**Tie-breakers:** 
1. Higher PerformanceScore -> 2. Lower CostPenalty -> 3. Earlier valid final submission time.

## Winning Strategy for ForgeLoop-AI
Because token count heavily penalizes the score (CostPenalty) and Autopilot grants a massive 15% bonus, ForgeLoop-AI's experiment engine should be calibrated to:
1.  **Iterative Meta-Prompting:** Use the human-in-the-loop (Copilot) approach *locally* during our experimental phases (EXP-XXX) to discover the optimal build instructions for a stable orbital rocket.
2.  **Autopilot Extraction:** Once we isolate the perfect build strategy, we compile it into a single, highly dense, token-efficient system/initial prompt.
3.  **Submission as Autopilot:** We execute the final official build as an **Autopilot** run to claim the x1.15 multiplier.
4.  **Tuning Mastery:** We heavily leverage the "legal human tuning" rule to perfect the flight controls post-generation, ensuring we hit the 20% speed bonus without requiring the AI to figure out exact control mappings.
