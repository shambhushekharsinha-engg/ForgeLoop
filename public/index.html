An advanced, auditable Human-AI Collaboration framework orchestrating the Besiege MCP. Featuring automated trajectory grading, mathematically optimized token scrubbing, and Autopilot prompt extraction for maximum Kaggle scoring.

Project Details & Methodology

The Scoring Math

The BuildArena challenge demands strict optimization. Our local evaluator grades identically to the official Kaggle parser: 70% Orbit Progress (up to 1080 degrees), 20% Speed Score, and 10% Structure Integrity.

Crucially, our architecture attacks the Cost Penalty. By using our custom TranscriptScrubber, we automatically regex-strip massive JSON XML geometries from the chat transcript before submission, mathematically minimizing our token footprint.

Copilot to Autopilot Pipeline

Instead of blind LLM generation, ForgeLoop is a rigorous engineering pipeline. We begin in Copilot Mode, manually logging AI structural proposals in our Decision Ledger (ACCEPT/REJECT) to discover stable aerospace geometry.

Once stability is achieved, our AutopilotCompiler compresses the winning strategy into a token-efficient mega-prompt. We run the final machine in Autopilot Mode to claim the competition's massive x1.15 multiplier.
